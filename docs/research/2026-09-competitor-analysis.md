# Competitive Analysis: Dutch- & Flemish-Language Downloadable DIY Pubquiz Websites

## TL;DR
- The market is crowded and mature in the Netherlands (15+ sellers) but thinner and structurally different in Flanders/Belgium, where the leading consumer players (Quizmas/Quizfabriek, Kwis.app) sell **streamed/web-app access** rather than downloadable PowerPoint/PDF files — Quizmas.be states plainly: *"Deze online quiz speel je met behulp van het QuizWitz-platform… Een internetverbinding om het quizpakket te streamen"* with *"🎟️ Koop een ticket voor het gekozen aantal deelnemers (of teams)"*. This leaves a genuine gap for a clean "download a PPT/PDF and play offline" product aimed at Belgian consumers.
- The near-universal formula is: instant email/download delivery of a PowerPoint (+PDF fallback), MP3 audio round(s), printable answer/score sheets and a quizmaster guide; 6–10 rounds; iDEAL/Bancontact via Mollie; a free sample quiz; theme quizzes (Kerst, familie, muziek); and prices clustering at two tiers — a budget/self-run tier (~€15–€25) and a premium multimedia/video tier (~€39–€59).
- To launch a compliant NL+BE bilingual shop, the true legal must-haves are: display of prices incl. VAT, KvK + BTW-ID (NL) / company + BTW (BE), a working digital-content withdrawal-right waiver (express consent + acknowledgement of loss of right at checkout), privacy/cookie compliance, and — new for the whole EU from **19 June 2026** — a prominent "cancellation button." Differentiators worth building: English versions, customisation (add your own questions/logo), a subscription, and strong social proof.

## Key Findings
1. **Two product architectures dominate.** (a) *Download model* — a ZIP with PowerPoint + PDF + MP3 + printable answer/score sheets + quizmaster instructions (PubQuiz Nederland, Quizt, Koning PubQuiz, Pubquizshop, PubQuizThuis, Quizkopen, Didgames, quiz-pub.nl). (b) *Streamed/app model* — you buy timed access and play in-browser or via an app with auto-scoring on players' phones (De Andere Quiz + app; QuizMaestro via Kahoot; OnlineQuiz via ClassicQuiz; Quizmas/Quizfabriek and Kwis.app in Belgium; Funtochten via its own app).
2. **The Belgian download niche is under-served.** The most visible Flemish consumer sellers (Quizmas.be, Quizfabriek.be, Kwis.app) are streaming-based; Quizkopen.be is the clearest download-PPT player, and it is a Belgian storefront of a Dutch brand. This is the single biggest whitespace.
3. **Pricing splits cleanly into tiers.** Budget download quizzes: ~€4.99–€25. Mid: PubQuiz Nederland ~€19.95–€24.95; PubQuizThuis from €15. Premium multimedia/video: De Andere Quiz — verbatim, *"Kies uit 2 opties: Silver (30 vragen) voor €39,- en Gold (60) voor maar €59,-!"* (Silver ≈45 min, Gold ≈90 min with 30+ video clips; files non-editable "om misbruik te voorkomen"). Belgian streamed tickets scale by player count (Quizmas €5–€30). Subscriptions exist (Quizt monthly/quarterly/yearly; De Andere Quiz horeca).
4. **Feature convergence is strong.** Nearly all offer: instant delivery, "no quizmaster required / you can play along," a free sample, printable answer sheets, a "how it works" page, FAQ, theme catalogue, and a private-use-only licence clause.
5. **Legal execution varies.** Best-practice sites (Koning PubQuiz) explicitly state the withdrawal right is waived because digital content is delivered immediately at the customer's express request; many smaller sites are sloppier.

### Common features (present on most sites)
- **Instant delivery** after payment (email download link or access link); "binnen 5 minuten."
- **Format core:** PowerPoint as primary, PDF as fallback ("geen PowerPoint? gebruik de PDF of Google Slides"), MP3 music round, printable answer + score sheets, quizmaster instructions/tips.
- **6–10 rounds**, ~40–90 questions, 1–2 hours; picture round + music round almost always included.
- **"No quizmaster needed / you can play along too."**
- **Free sample/mini-quiz** as a lead magnet (PubQuiz Nederland, Quizt, Koning PubQuiz; free templates).
- **Theme catalogue:** Kerst, Eindejaars/Oud & Nieuw, Familie/gezin, Muziek/Top 2000, Sport/Voetbal, decades (60s/90s), kids, vrijgezellenfeest/babyshower.
- **Payments via Mollie:** iDEAL, Bancontact, credit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, KBC/CBC.
- **Private-use-only licence** clause ("niet commercieel exploiteren / delen").
- **Social proof:** Trustpilot / Google reviews, testimonials; FAQ; "how it works" 3-step; About page.
- **SEO content engine:** free quiz-question banks, "how to host a pubquiz" guides, blog.

### Notable differentiators / exceptions
- **Auto-scoring apps** (De Andere Quiz, QuizMaestro/Kahoot, Funtochten) remove manual marking.
- **Editable/branded quizzes** (OnlineQuiz editable version; QuizMaestro "add 10 own questions"; Quizt maatwerk) — personalisation.
- **Subscriptions** (Quizt monthly/quarter/half/year; De Andere Quiz horeca) — recurring revenue, mainly aimed at cafés/venues.
- **Multi-language beyond EN**: Groepsspellen offers German and Italian; Pubquizdownload offers mixed NL/EN.
- **Belgium-specific streamed models** (QuizWitz, web-app) show Flemish consumers are used to buying time-boxed access, not files.
- **Physical-post option** (Quiz Brothers; Pubquiz.nl) — outside the pure-digital scope but present.

### Legal requirements for selling digital products to NL & BE consumers
- **VAT-inclusive pricing:** consumer prices must always be shown incl. VAT; all extra costs disclosed up front.
- **Business identification:** NL requires KvK number and BTW-ID visible on the website and correspondence; BE requires company name and BTW number on site and invoices.
- **Withdrawal right (herroepingsrecht):** default 14-day right for distance contracts. For digital content NOT on a material carrier it can be excluded, but ONLY if the consumer (a) gives **prior express consent** to immediate delivery and (b) **acknowledges losing** the withdrawal right, and (c) the trader **confirms** this. A checkbox at checkout plus confirmation in the order email is the standard mechanism. This must be in the terms AND actively agreed — a terms mention alone is insufficient. In BE the basis is Art. VI.53, 13° WER (mirroring EU Directive 2011/83/EU art. 16(m)); note that for streamed/access (service-like) products the right only lapses after full performance. Best-practice wording is visible on koningpubquiz.nl: *"Omdat de dienst direct geleverd wordt, en wel op uw uitdrukkelijk verzoek, is het niet mogelijk een betaling ongedaan te maken met een beroep op de Wet koop op afstand."*
- **Cancellation button (new):** Directive (EU) 2023/2673 added a new **Article 11a** to the Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU. Per Loyens & Loeff (via Lexology): *"Member States were required to transpose the Directive into national law by 19 December 2025, with the new obligations applying as from 19 June 2026. At the time of writing, Belgium has not yet transposed the Directive into national law."* From 19 June 2026 every EU webshop offering a withdrawal right must display a clearly labelled cancellation/withdrawal button.
- **Cost of non-compliance:** omitting the withdrawal information triggers, per merchant guidance (ShippyPro/Shopify Help Center), *"an automatic extension of the withdrawal window to 12 months and 14 days,"* plus potential *"fines of up to 4% of annual turnover in some EU member states."*
- **Other:** legally compliant terms & conditions, a returns/withdrawal page, privacy statement (AVG/GDPR), cookie consent, statutory conformity/warranty (cannot be excluded), and a model withdrawal form.

## Recommendations

**Positioning (do this first).** Build a **bilingual (NL + EN) download-model shop** and explicitly target **Belgium as well as the Netherlands**, because the Flemish market's leaders sell streamed access, leaving the clean "download a polished PowerPoint + PDF + audio + printable sheets, play offline forever" product under-served there. Make Flemish-localised content (a "Vlaamse quiz," Belgian Kerst/familie editions) a first-class category, not an afterthought.

**Staged build:**
1. **MVP (launch):** 6–10 rounds per quiz; PowerPoint primary + PDF fallback + MP3 audio + printable answer/score sheets + quizmaster guide, delivered instantly by email link; a free sample quiz as lead magnet; Mollie with iDEAL + Bancontact + cards + Apple/Google Pay; "how it works" 3-step page, FAQ, About, reviews widget; all legal pages (see checklist) with a working withdrawal-waiver checkbox; VAT-inclusive prices; the 19-June-2026 cancellation button. Price at two tiers: entry ~€15–€19.95 and a larger/premium edition ~€24.95–€29.95.
2. **Phase 2 (differentiate):** English versions of every quiz; customisation ("add your own questions / logo"); theme expansion (Kerst, Eindejaars, Familie, Muziek/Top 2000, kids, vrijgezellen); a monthly/annual subscription for repeat hosts and venues.
3. **Phase 3 (moat):** auto-scoring option (app or Kahoot/ClassicQuiz-style) for buyers who want no manual marking; SEO content engine (free question banks + "host a pubquiz" guides) to capture the high-intent "pubquiz downloaden/kopen" searches the incumbents dominate.

### Required vs. Optional checklist

**REQUIRED (must-have)**
- Prices displayed **incl. VAT**; all extra costs shown before checkout.
- **KvK + BTW-ID** (NL) / **company name + BTW number** (BE) on site + invoices; contact details (email + address).
- **Working withdrawal-waiver flow** for digital content: checkbox for express consent to immediate delivery + acknowledgement of losing the right, confirmed in the order email.
- **Legal pages:** terms & conditions, privacy statement (AVG/GDPR), cookie consent, returns/withdrawal page + model withdrawal form.
- **Cancellation/withdrawal button** (Art. 11a, mandatory from 19 June 2026).
- **Instant digital delivery** (download/access link by email) + invoice.
- **Core product contents:** PowerPoint + PDF fallback + audio + printable answer/score sheets + quizmaster instructions.
- **Secure payment** with iDEAL (NL) and Bancontact (BE) — Mollie covers both.
- **Private-use licence clause** in product/terms.

**OPTIONAL (nice-to-have / differentiators)**
- English (and later German) versions.
- Free sample/mini-quiz lead magnet + newsletter capture.
- Customisation: add-your-own-questions, branding/logo, maatwerk.
- Subscription plan (monthly/annual) for repeat buyers and venues.
- Auto-scoring app / Kahoot- or QuizWitz-style option.
- Trust marks (Trustpilot/WebwinkelKeur), testimonials, review widgets.
- SEO content: free question banks, host-a-quiz guides, blog.
- Bundles (e.g. Christmas pack), gift options, "spoed/instant" category.

**Benchmarks that would change the plan:** if conversion on the download tier is weak but Belgian traffic is strong, add a streamed/QuizWitz-style option to match local expectations; if repeat-purchase rate is low, prioritise the subscription; if English traffic converts, expand to German (as Groepsspellen does).

## Caveats
- Prices move and several sites run permanent "aanbieding" discounts; treat all figures as indicative snapshots (Sept 2026), not fixed.
- Quizkopen.be's prices could not be directly verified (site blocks automated access); its €25–€29.95 range is inferred from the Dutch sibling brand.
- Funtochten.be uses a Dutch phone/address despite the .be domain — it is Dutch-operated but targets Belgium.
- Streamed vs. download distinction has real legal consequences (service vs. digital content) for how the withdrawal-right waiver applies; take specific legal advice before finalising checkout wording.
- The 19-June-2026 cancellation-button obligation stems from an EU directive with a fixed application date; national transposition in Belgium was reported as still outstanding at the research date, but the EU application date itself is firm.
